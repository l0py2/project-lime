## Ore farming

You can generate basic raw ore blocks like crimsite (block used to obtain iron)
by going to the bedrock layer of a specific biome and making a cobblestone generator
above a bedrock block.

Sometimes the **result can be different**.

| Biome    | Block    | Ore    |
| ---      | ---      | ---    |
| Plains   | Crimsite | Iron   |
| Taiga    | Asurine  | Zinc   |
| Ocean    | Veridium | Copper |
| Badlands | Ochrum   | Gold   |
